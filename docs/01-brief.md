# Salon Queue Management System — Project Brief & Scope

## Problem
Customers often wait at a salon without knowing their queue position or approximate waiting time. Reception staff must coordinate walk-ins and several barbers manually, which can lead to confusion about queue order and barber preferences.

The Salon Queue Management System lets customers join and track a live queue while receptionists and barbers manage service progress from connected screens.

## Users
- **Customer:** joins the queue, selects services and a preferred barber, and tracks their token and estimated wait.
- **Receptionist:** manages the waiting list, adds walk-ins, calls customers, and handles cancellations and no-shows.
- **Barber:** views their assigned customer, starts and completes service, and updates availability.
- **Salon owner:** manages services, barber duty status and online queue availability, and views daily statistics.

## Must-have features
1. Customers can join from a phone by entering their name and 10-digit phone number, selecting up to three services, and choosing a specific barber or Any Barber.
2. Each customer receives a queue token and a live status page showing queue position and estimated waiting time.
3. Receptionists can view the queue, add walk-in customers, call the next eligible customer for a barber, and mark customers as No-show or Cancelled.
4. Barbers have an individual view to see the current customer, start and finish service, and switch between Available and On break.
5. A waiting-area display shows the customer currently with each barber and the next five tokens.
6. Owners can enable or disable services, edit service duration and price, manage which barbers are on duty, and open or close online joining.
7. Queue changes are synchronized across customer, reception, barber and display screens, with updates targeted within five seconds.
8. The customer-facing interface is mobile-first and uses English labels throughout.

## Don't build
- Online payments.
- Future-date appointments or appointment booking.
- SMS or WhatsApp messaging.
- Loyalty points or rewards.
- Multiple salon branches.
- Features outside the queue-management workflow unless approved first.

## Look & feel
A premium, dark-luxury salon interface using deep charcoal/near-black surfaces, warm gold accents, refined typography, clear status colours and large mobile-friendly controls; English only.
